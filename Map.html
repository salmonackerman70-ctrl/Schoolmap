ABOUT

QR-Based Campus Navigation System

This image shows the first part of our QR-Based Campus Navigation System project.

The system is designed to help students and visitors easily find important locations around the campus.

QR codes can be used to provide quick access to information about different areas of the school.

The campus map helps users understand the location of buildings, rooms, and other important areas.

This project aims to make campus navigation easier, faster, and more convenient for new students and visitors.
